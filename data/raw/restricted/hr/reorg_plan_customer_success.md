# Customer success reorganization plan

Classification: Restricted

Last reviewed: 2026-09-10 by Aisha Karim (Head of People) and Nina Castellanos (Head of Product)

## Why this is changing

Customer success currently reports through a single director structure built when we had roughly 600 customers. At 1,148 customers and 112 percent net revenue retention, the team is stretched unevenly: Enterprise accounts get white glove attention while a growing tail of Starter and Growth tier customers gets thin, reactive support. This plan restructures customer success into segmented pods and is planned to take effect November 2, 2026.

This document is restricted because it discusses specific personnel moves, a role elimination, and management changes that have not been communicated to affected employees yet. Any leak before the planned announcement date would be damaging and unfair to the people involved, who deserve to hear it directly.

## New structure

Customer success moves from one flat team of 61 reporting to a single director, to three pods:

- Enterprise pod (dedicated to our top roughly 80 accounts by ARR), led by a new director hire (see hr/headcount_plan_2027.md).
- Growth pod, covering the mid-market tier, led by current senior manager Priya Nakamura (promoted to director as part of this reorg).
- Scaled pod, covering Starter tier and long tail Growth accounts, using a more automated, one-to-many support model, led by current manager Devon Ashby.

Reporting structure moves from customer success reporting to VP Sales (Jordan Pike) to reporting to Nina Castellanos as Head of Product, on the reasoning that renewal and expansion should be closer to the product roadmap. Jordan Pike has agreed to this change; it was not without disagreement internally and reflects several weeks of debate at the leadership level.

## Personnel impact

One current role, the customer success operations manager position held by Casey Whitfield, is being eliminated as part of the restructuring, with responsibilities absorbed into the new pod lead structure and a smaller operations function under Nina Castellanos. Aisha Karim is managing a standard severance package for this role, consistent with our policy (two weeks per year of tenure, minimum four weeks), and this conversation is scheduled for the week of October 26, ahead of the public announcement.

Two other individual contributors are being moved from generalist customer success roles into the new Scaled pod, a lateral move with no compensation change. These moves have not been discussed with the affected employees as of this review and should not be referenced in any conversation with them before Aisha Karim and their new manager do so directly.

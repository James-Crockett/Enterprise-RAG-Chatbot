# 2027 headcount plan

Classification: Restricted

Last reviewed: 2026-08-20 by Aisha Karim (Head of People) and Elena Vasquez (CFO)

## Context

This plan sets planned headcount for 2027, built against the revenue forecast that assumes we exit 2026 near $78M ARR and target $102M by end of 2027. Current headcount as of August 2026 is 428. Planned end of 2027 headcount is 512, an increase of 84, or about 20 percent, well below our revenue growth target, reflecting an assumption of continued efficiency gains per headcount.

This document is restricted because it includes specific new roles and locations not yet announced, and because the numbers below would tell a competitor a lot about where we are investing.

## By location

Austin remains the largest office and takes most of the G&A and executive growth. Toronto grows moderately in engineering (planned 12 of the 30 engineering adds), supporting the geo-tiles and mobile driver app teams. Lisbon is the fastest growing office in percentage terms, planned to go from 44 to 68 people, mostly customer success and a new support hub covering European hours, plus a small security engineering pod reporting to Tom Brennan.

We are not opening a new office location in 2027. A Latin America remote hiring pilot for customer success is under discussion for late 2027 but is not yet approved and should not be referenced outside this document until Dana Okafor and Aisha Karim confirm it.

## Notable new roles

- A second VP-level hire is planned in customer success by Q2 2027, reporting to Nina Castellanos, ahead of any formal reorg announcement. See hr/reorg_plan_customer_success.md for how this interacts with the current reorg.
- Security and IT grows by 7, including a dedicated compliance engineer role to support SOC 2 and upcoming ISO 27001 work, and two additional security engineers under Tom Brennan.
- Sales adds a first dedicated sales engineering function of 6 people by mid 2027, separate from the account executive headcount, to support increasingly technical Enterprise deals.

## Attrition assumption

This plan assumes voluntary attrition holds at 11 percent annualized, in line with 2025 and H1 2026 actuals. If attrition rises above 14 percent, gross hiring needs increase by roughly 15 to 20 additional hires to hit the same net plan, which would require additional budget beyond what is currently modeled.

## Approval and confidentiality

Location and new role detail, and anything about the Latin America pilot, stays within the executive team and Aisha Karim's direct reports until a public hiring plan is communicated in Q4 2026.

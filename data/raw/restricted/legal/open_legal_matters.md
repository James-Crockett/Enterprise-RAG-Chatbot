## Litigation and disputes

Elena Vasquez has been informed for reserve purposes.

**Former employee wage claim, Toronto office.** A former customer success representative, terminated in April 2026, filed a claim with the Ontario Ministry of Labour alleging improper classification of overtime hours. Aisha Karim and Ruth Adeyemi are coordinating with local employment counsel. Estimated exposure is modest, under CAD 40,000, but the matter is being handled carefully because two other current Toronto employees raised similar informal questions about overtime tracking in the past year, and the classification issue may need to be corrected more broadly regardless of the outcome of this specific claim.

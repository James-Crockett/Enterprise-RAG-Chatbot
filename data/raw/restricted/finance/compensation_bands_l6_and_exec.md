# Compensation bands, L6 and above

Classification: Restricted

Last reviewed: 2026-03-03 by Aisha Karim (Head of People) and Elena Vasquez (CFO)

## Scope

This document extends the individual contributor and manager bands (L3 through L5, documented separately) with L6, director, and VP level bands, plus the equity refresh guidelines for all levels. It is restricted because it exposes exact pay ranges and equity math that we do not disclose broadly, even internally, ahead of level.

For reference, the existing bands: L3 $90,000 to $120,000, L4 $120,000 to $160,000, L5 $160,000 to $220,000.

## L6 and director bands

| Level | Base salary range | Target bonus | New hire equity (bps) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L6 (staff / senior manager) | 220,000 to 270,000 | 10% | 15 to 30 |
| Director | 240,000 to 300,000 | 15% | 25 to 45 |
| Senior director | 270,000 to 330,000 | 15% | 35 to 60 |

Bps means basis points of fully diluted shares at time of grant, subject to a four year vest with a one year cliff. Legal counsel Ruth Adeyemi should review any grant outside these ranges before it goes to the board comp committee.

## Equity refresh guidelines

Refresh grants are evaluated annually in Q3, effective October 1. Eligibility requires at least 18 months of tenure and a performance rating of "meets" or above. Refresh sizing targets bringing an employee's unvested equity value back to roughly 60 percent of a new hire grant at their level, based on the most recent 409A valuation.

| Level band | Typical refresh size (bps) |
| --- | --- |
| L3 to L5 | 3 to 12 |
| L6 to director | 10 to 25 |
| VP and above | 20 to 50 |

The Q3 2026 refresh cycle budget is capped at 180 bps in aggregate across the company, allocated by Aisha Karim and Elena Vasquez with sign off from Dana Okafor. Managers should not promise refresh amounts to reports before the cycle is finalized in September.

## Exceptions

Any offer or adjustment outside these bands requires written approval from both Aisha Karim and Elena Vasquez, logged in the comp exceptions tracker. Exceptions above $20,000 total comp variance also require Dana Okafor's sign off. We have granted four such exceptions since January 2026, all tied to competing offers for senior engineering talent in the Toronto office.

# H2 2026 revenue forecast

Classification: Restricted

Last reviewed: 2026-07-15 by Elena Vasquez (CFO)

## Where we stand

ARR closed Q2 2026 at $64.1M, against an internal plan of $65.5M. We are $1.4M behind plan, driven mostly by two Enterprise deals (Pinecrest Freight and Harbor Lane Couriers, roughly $900k combined ARR) that slipped from Q2 into Q3. Net revenue retention held at 112 percent, which is the number giving the board the most confidence that H2 is recoverable.

Gross margin for Q2 was 76.1 percent, in line with plan. Customer count at end of Q2 was 1,148.

## Full year target and the gap to close

The board approved target is $78M ARR by December 31, 2026. Closing from $64.1M to $78M means $13.9M of net new ARR in six months, roughly 60 percent more per quarter than we added in H1. This forecast lays out how we get there and where it could fail.

| Quarter | New ARR plan | Cumulative ARR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q3 2026 | 7,200 | 71,300 |
| Q4 2026 | 6,700 | 78,000 |

## Pipeline supporting the plan

Jordan Pike's team has $19.4M of qualified Enterprise pipeline for H2, weighted at 42 percent to reach the $13.9M target. That weighting is aggressive against our historical Enterprise close rate of 27 percent, and this is the single biggest risk to the plan. If close rates revert to historical norms, we land closer to $73M, roughly $5M under the board number.

Three accounts make up $4.1M of the H2 pipeline on their own: a national grocery distribution account, a regional parcel network in the Midwest, and a cold chain logistics group evaluating us against a legacy competitor. Losing any one of these moves the full year number meaningfully, which is why Dana Okafor is personally involved in the grocery distribution deal.

## Downside scenario

If the Enterprise slippage pattern from Q2 repeats in Q3, we forecast landing the year at $74.5M to $76M rather than $78M. Elena Vasquez has told the board this range is the realistic floor, not the target, and that a revision would only be communicated externally if we are below $75M by end of Q3.

Do not share the downside numbers outside the executive team and board finance committee. Sales leadership should continue operating against the $78M target.

## Cost of the push

Hitting the upside case requires the sales headcount additions funded in the 2026 budget to be fully ramped by August 1, plus roughly $600k of incremental deal desk discounting authority for Jordan Pike to close slipped Enterprise deals before quarter end. Elena Vasquez has approved this discount pool on a deal by deal basis only, capped at 15 percent off list for Enterprise contracts over $150k ARR.

## Assumptions to revisit

This forecast assumes no material churn beyond the 112 percent NRR baseline, no further FX movement affecting the Lisbon and Toronto cost base, and that the Growth tier price increase planned for Q4 2026 (Starter and Growth list prices unchanged for now) does not materially slow new logo signups. We will revisit all three assumptions at the September board meeting.

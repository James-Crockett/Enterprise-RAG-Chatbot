## Cascade Cold Chain, high risk detail

Cascade Cold Chain's renewal is at risk because their VP of Operations, our internal champion, left the company in June 2026, and the new operations lead has been in active conversations with a competitor (evaluating a rip and replace of route-api and dispatch-worker). Jordan Pike has personally taken over the relationship. Our current negotiating position is to offer a 12 percent multi-year discount in exchange for a three year commitment and a case study, but we have not yet offered this to the customer. Do not disclose this discount ceiling to the account team below Jordan Pike's direct reports, since it sets a floor that could leak into other renewal conversations.

## Harbor Lane Couriers, contract detail

Harbor Lane's contract includes a most favored nation pricing clause negotiated in 2024, which obligates us to extend any better pricing we give to a similarly sized customer. This clause has constrained our ability to offer aggressive discounts to Cascade Cold Chain and Delta Parcel Network without triggering a pricing conversation with Harbor Lane. Ruth Adeyemi is reviewing whether the clause applies to the Cascade discount under discussion; legal sign off is required before that offer goes out.

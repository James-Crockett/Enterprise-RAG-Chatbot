# Hiring process at Kestrel Systems

Last reviewed: 2026-01-10

This page explains what to expect if you apply for a role at Kestrel Systems.

## Overview

Most roles follow the same general structure: an application review, a recruiter screen, one or more interviews with the hiring team, and a final decision. Timelines vary by role and team, but most candidates hear back within 2 weeks of each stage.

## Stages

1. **Application review.** A recruiter reviews your application against the role's requirements. If it looks like a fit, you will hear from us within 5 business days.

2. **Recruiter screen.** A 30 minute call covering your background, what you are looking for, and an overview of the role and Kestrel Systems. This is also your chance to ask questions about the team or the company.

3. **Hiring manager interview.** A conversation with the person you would report to, focused on your relevant experience and how it maps to the role.

4. **Team interviews.** Depending on the role, this may include a technical exercise (for engineering roles), a case study or work sample (for product, sales, or marketing roles), or a panel of interviews with future teammates and cross functional partners.

5. **Final decision and offer.** After the team debriefs, the hiring manager and Aisha Karim's People team finalize the decision. If you receive an offer, a recruiter will walk you through compensation, start date, and next steps.

## What we look for

Beyond role specific skills, we look for people who can work well with a distributed team across Austin, Toronto, and Lisbon, communicate clearly in writing, and are comfortable with ambiguity, since Kestrel Systems is still a growing company at around 420 employees.

## Interview logistics

Interviews are conducted over video call. If you need accommodations for any part of the process, let your recruiter know and we will work with you to arrange them.

## After you apply

You can expect updates from your recruiter at each stage of the process, even if the update is that we need more time. If we decide not to move forward, we will let you know rather than leaving your application open ended.

## Once you join

New hires set up their company email and multi factor authentication on day one, read through the employee handbook, and join the relevant Slack channels for their team. Security training is required in the first week, and access to specific tools (Salesforce, Jira, Confluence, and others depending on role) is requested through the internal access portal once your manager confirms what you need.

Office days follow business casual dress. Office hours are 9am to 5pm local time across Austin, Toronto, and Lisbon, and major federal holidays are observed based on your work location.

## Questions

If you have questions about an open role or the process in general, reach out to the recruiter listed on the job posting, or email careers@kestrelsystems.com.

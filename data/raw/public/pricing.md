# Kestrel Route pricing

Last reviewed: 2026-02-01

Kestrel Route is priced per vehicle per month. All tiers include onboarding support and access to the help portal.

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | Vehicle limit | Modules included | Uptime SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49 per vehicle per month | Up to 25 vehicles | Route Planner, Driver App | 99.9 percent |
| Growth | $89 per vehicle per month | No fixed limit | Route Planner, Driver App, Live Dispatch, Proof of Delivery, API access | 99.9 percent |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing | No fixed limit | All modules, single sign on, EU data residency, dedicated CSM | 99.95 percent |

## What is included at each tier

Starter is built for smaller fleets that need route planning and driver navigation without live dispatch tracking. It is capped at 25 vehicles; fleets that grow past that move to Growth.

Growth adds live dispatch visibility, proof of delivery capture, and access to the public API (600 requests per minute per API key) for teams that want to connect their own systems.

Enterprise is for fleets that need single sign on for their organization, EU data residency (data hosted in AWS eu-west-1 instead of us-east-1), a 99.95 percent uptime service level agreement, and a dedicated customer success manager. Enterprise pricing depends on fleet size, contract term, and which integrations are in scope, so contact sales for a quote.

## Integrations

Salesforce, NetSuite, and Shopify integrations are available on Growth and Enterprise. Samsara telematics integration is available on all tiers. The public API is available on Growth and Enterprise.

## Annual contracts

Customers who commit to an annual contract, rather than paying month to month, receive 15 percent off list price on Starter and Growth. Enterprise annual pricing is negotiated as part of the custom quote.

## Support

Starter and Growth customers reach support through the help portal, where a ticket is opened and tracked. Enterprise customers have the same help portal access plus a named customer success manager for account level questions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I mix tiers within one fleet?** No. Pricing applies at the account level, based on your total vehicle count and the modules you need across the fleet.

**What happens if I go over 25 vehicles on Starter?** You will need to move to Growth. Your CSM or account rep can help with the transition, and existing route and driver data carries over.

**Is there a setup fee?** No standard setup fee for Starter or Growth. Enterprise onboarding scope (data migration, custom integration work) is discussed as part of the quote.

**Can I cancel at any time?** Month to month Starter and Growth contracts can be cancelled with 30 days notice. Annual contracts run for the committed term; contact your account rep about early termination terms.

**Do you offer a trial?** Sales can set up a sandbox trial for qualified prospects. Contact sales through the website to discuss availability and scope.

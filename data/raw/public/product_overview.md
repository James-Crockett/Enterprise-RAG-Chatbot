# Kestrel Route product overview

Last reviewed: 2026-02-01

Kestrel Route is a route planning and dispatch platform built for logistics fleets, including last mile delivery, field service, and regional freight operations.

## Modules

**Route Planner.** Plans multi stop routes for a fleet, accounting for vehicle capacity, time windows, and driver shifts. Supports manual reordering of stops after a route is generated.

**Live Dispatch.** A real time view of vehicle location and stop status across the fleet, with alerts for late stops, route deviations, and vehicles that go offline.

**Driver App.** A mobile app for iOS and Android that gives drivers turn by turn navigation, stop details, and a way to update stop status from the road.

**Proof of Delivery.** Captures a signature, a photo, or both at the point of delivery, and attaches it to the stop record for later reference.

**Analytics.** Reporting on on time delivery rate, driver idle time, and other operational metrics, broken out by region or depot.

## Integrations

Kestrel Route integrates with Salesforce, NetSuite, and Shopify for order and customer data, and with Samsara for vehicle telematics. A public API is available for teams that want to build custom integrations, with a rate limit of 600 requests per minute per API key.

## Who uses it

Fleet operations teams, dispatchers, and drivers use Kestrel Route day to day. IT and operations leaders typically own the purchasing decision, often together with a finance stakeholder for larger contracts.

## Pricing tiers

Kestrel Route is priced per vehicle per month. Starter includes Route Planner and Driver App for fleets up to 25 vehicles. Growth adds Live Dispatch, Proof of Delivery, and API access. Enterprise is custom priced and adds single sign on, EU data residency, and a dedicated customer success manager. See the pricing page for current rates and details.

## Support

All customers can reach support through the help portal, where a ticket is created and tracked through resolution. Response times vary by issue severity and by tier, with Enterprise customers on a 99.95 percent uptime service level agreement and a dedicated customer success manager for day to day questions.

## Getting started

New customers are set up by their assigned CSM (Enterprise) or by the support team (Starter and Growth), typically starting with a data import (vehicles, drivers, and standing delivery or service locations), followed by a short onboarding call to walk through Route Planner and the Driver App. Live Dispatch and Proof of Delivery, where included, are typically introduced in a second onboarding session once route planning is running smoothly.

## Security and compliance

Kestrel Systems maintains SOC 2 Type II certification, renewed annually, and is GDPR compliant for customers with EU data. See the security and privacy page for details on data hosting, retention, and access controls.

## Company

Kestrel Systems is based in Austin, Texas, with additional offices in Toronto and Lisbon. The company was founded in 2018 and has about 420 employees as of this writing.

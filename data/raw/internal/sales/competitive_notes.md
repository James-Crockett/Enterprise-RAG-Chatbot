# Competitive notes

Last reviewed: 2026-02-25

Owner: Jordan Pike, VP Sales, with input from Nina Castellanos, Head of Product. Source: Gong call tags and win/loss interviews.

## Main competitors

**Fernway Logistics Software.** The competitor we see most often in Growth tier deals, roughly 20 to 100 vehicles. Fernway's routing engine is comparable to ours on basic stop sequencing, but they do not have a native driver mobile app. Their customers use a third party app, which reps say is a common source of frustration in win/loss calls. Fernway prices per vehicle similarly to our Growth tier but caps API calls at 100 requests per minute, well under our 600 per minute limit. Reps should lead with the driver app and API limit when Fernway is in the deal.

**Northstar Dispatch.** Shows up in Enterprise deals, particularly with field service companies rather than delivery fleets. Northstar has stronger scheduling for appointment based field service work, and this is a real gap for us. Do not claim parity on appointment scheduling. If a prospect needs tight appointment windows and recurring service scheduling as a primary use case, flag it to Nina Castellanos's team since this comes up often enough to be a roadmap input. Northstar's uptime SLA is 99.9 percent across all their tiers, so our 99.95 percent Enterprise SLA is a real differentiator there.

**Cartway.** A newer, cheaper entrant, usually seen with Starter tier prospects under 25 vehicles. Cartway undercuts on price but has had two public outages in the past year that came up in customer references. Cartway does not have a Proof of Delivery module. Do not get pulled into a pure price war with Cartway. Reps should reframe on reliability and the fact that a customer would need to add a second tool for proof of delivery.

**Relay Freight OS.** Mostly a regional freight competitor, less common in last mile delivery deals. Strong NetSuite integration, comparable to ours. Weaker on live dispatch, since their dispatch view updates on a 5 minute refresh rather than real time. This has come up as a deciding factor in at least 3 deals per Gong tags in the past two quarters.

## Positioning summary

| Competitor | Their strength | Our angle |
|---|---|---|
| Fernway | Price parity at Growth tier | Native driver app, higher API limit |
| Northstar | Field service scheduling | Higher uptime SLA, stronger delivery focused routing |
| Cartway | Lowest price | Reliability, proof of delivery included |
| Relay Freight OS | NetSuite integration | Real time dispatch view |

## Handling "why not build it ourselves"

This comes up most with larger prospects that have an internal engineering team. The standard response is to walk through total cost of ownership: routing algorithm maintenance, telematics integrations (Samsara in particular), and mobile app maintenance across iOS and Android add up to a multi year build for most teams. Ask how the prospect's engineering roadmap would change if routing and dispatch were off their plate.

## What not to say

Do not disparage a competitor by name in writing that could end up in a customer's inbox or a public forum. Verbal positioning on a call is fine within the guidance above. Do not claim a specific competitor's uptime or pricing numbers unless they are current, since competitor pricing changes and unverified claims create legal risk. When in doubt, check with Jordan Pike before citing a specific competitor number in a proposal.

# Sales playbook

Last reviewed: 2026-03-02

Owner: Jordan Pike, VP Sales.

## Who we sell to

Kestrel Route sells to logistics operations leaders and IT leaders at companies running delivery, field service, or regional freight fleets. Our best fit customer runs between 15 and 300 vehicles, has at least one person who owns routing or dispatch full time, and is currently either doing manual routing in spreadsheets or using an older, unsupported routing tool.

Bad fit signals: fewer than 10 vehicles (push to Starter tier or decline), fleets that are mostly long haul trucking with drop and hook (Route Planner is not built for that), or a buyer who wants a fully custom on premise deployment.

## Qualification

Use these questions on the first call.

1. How many vehicles are you routing today, and is that number growing.
2. What are you using now (spreadsheets, a competitor, in house software).
3. Who besides you would need to approve a new tool, and what is the budget cycle.
4. Do you need Salesforce, NetSuite, Shopify, or Samsara telematics integration.
5. Is EU data residency a requirement (this usually means Enterprise tier).

## Deal stages in Salesforce

Discovery, Technical Validation, Proposal, Negotiation, Closed Won or Closed Lost. Every stage change should have a note in Salesforce with the next step and date. Gong call recordings should be tagged with the deal stage so Nina Castellanos's product team can pull competitive mentions later.

## Demo guidance

Lead with Route Planner and Live Dispatch, since those show the clearest before and after for a prospect doing manual routing. Only demo Proof of Delivery and Analytics if the prospect asks or if discovery surfaced a specific pain point (missed delivery disputes, no visibility into driver performance).

Do not demo the public API unless the prospect's IT lead is on the call. API access is a Growth and Enterprise feature; showing it to a Starter prospect creates confusion about what is included.

## Tier fit

Starter fits fleets under 25 vehicles that only need route planning and the driver app. Growth fits fleets that need live dispatch, proof of delivery, or API access, most commonly 25 to 150 vehicles. Enterprise fits anyone needing SSO, EU data residency, the 99.95 percent uptime SLA, or a dedicated CSM, regardless of fleet size, though in practice this is usually 100+ vehicles.

Do not undersell an Enterprise fit customer into Growth to close faster. It creates support and security escalations later and Tom Brennan's team will flag it.

## Objection handling

"Your competitor is cheaper." Ask what specifically they are comparing. Most competitor quotes at this price point exclude live dispatch or cap API calls well below our 600 requests per minute per key limit. Reframe on total cost including the manual work their team is doing today.

"We need to see it work with our own data first." Offer a sandbox trial. Standard trial length is 14 days and covers up to 10 vehicles on the Growth tier feature set at no cost. Trials past 14 days need VP Sales approval.

"Can you match this competitor's uptime guarantee." Point to Enterprise's 99.95 percent SLA, and be direct that Starter and Growth are 99.9 percent. Do not promise a custom SLA without Elena Vasquez and Ruth Adeyemi involved.

## Handoff to Customer Success

For Enterprise deals, loop in the assigned CSM at Proposal stage, not after signature, so they can join the final call and start the relationship warm. For Growth and Starter, the CSM (or the support team, for Starter) is introduced by email within 2 business days of Closed Won.

# Discount approval process

Last reviewed: 2026-01-08

Owner: Elena Vasquez, CFO. Process owner for day to day approvals: Jordan Pike, VP Sales.

## Standard discounts

Annual contracts (paid upfront or on an annual invoice schedule) get 15 percent off list price automatically. This is built into the Salesforce quote template and does not need separate approval. This applies to Starter and Growth. Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated per deal, so the 15 percent annual discount is a starting reference point, not a fixed rule.

## Approval thresholds

| Total discount off list price | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to 15 percent | No approval needed (standard annual discount) |
| 15.01 to 25 percent | Sales manager approval, noted in the Salesforce opportunity |
| 25.01 to 35 percent | Jordan Pike (VP Sales) approval required |
| Above 35 percent | Elena Vasquez (CFO) approval required, in addition to Jordan Pike |

Discount here means the total off list price after stacking any promo, multi year commitment, or one time concession. If a rep stacks a 15 percent annual discount with an extra 15 percent concession, that is 30 percent total and needs VP Sales approval, not just manager sign off.

## How to request approval

Reps submit the discount request as a comment on the Salesforce opportunity, tagging the approver, with the deal size, discount percentage, and the reason (competitive pressure, multi year commitment, strategic logo, budget constraint). Jordan Pike or Elena Vasquez responds in Salesforce, and the approval is copied into the quote notes before the order form goes to legal review.

Verbal approval in a Slack DM does not count. If an approver gives a quick yes in Slack, the rep still needs to log it on the opportunity before generating the order form.

## Multi year deals

Multi year contracts (2 or 3 year terms) can carry an additional 5 percent off per year beyond year one, up to a combined cap of 30 percent, without needing CFO approval, as long as the base annual discount plus the multi year add on stays at or under 30 percent. Anything above that follows the standard threshold table.

## Non standard requests

Some asks are not pure percentage discounts and route through this same table based on their revenue impact:

- Free months (for example, 2 months free on a 12 month term): treat as the equivalent percentage discount for approval purposes.
- Waived onboarding or implementation fees: manager approval regardless of deal size, since these are usually small dollar amounts, but check with Elena Vasquez's team if the fee exceeds $10,000.
- Price holds for a renewal (holding a customer's current price through a list price increase): sales manager approval, since this is common for renewals.

## What approvers look for

Jordan Pike checks whether the discount is justified by deal size, competitive situation, or strategic value, and whether it sets a bad precedent for a customer segment. Elena Vasquez checks margin impact and whether the deal still clears the company's target gross margin for that tier.

## Audit

Elena Vasquez's team reviews closed deals quarterly to confirm every discount above 15 percent had a logged approval before signature. Deals found without proper approval are flagged to the rep's manager and included in the quarterly sales ops review.

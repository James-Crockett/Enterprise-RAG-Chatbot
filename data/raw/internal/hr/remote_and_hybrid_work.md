# Remote and hybrid work policy

Last reviewed: 2026-02-01 by Aisha Karim, Head of People

## Work models

Kestrel Systems supports three work arrangements depending on role and office location:

| Location | Model |
| --- | --- |
| Austin | Hybrid, Tuesday to Thursday in office |
| Toronto | Hybrid, Tuesday to Thursday in office |
| Lisbon | Fully flexible, no fixed in-office days |
| Fully remote roles (any location) | Remote, no regular office requirement |

Hybrid staff in Austin and Toronto are expected in the office Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday are flexible, whether that means working from home or coming in anyway.

## Office hours

Standard office hours are 9am to 5pm local time, regardless of work model. This is when core collaboration happens and when you should be reachable on Slack. Outside those hours, respond at your own discretion.

## Home office setup

Employees on a hybrid or remote model get:

- A one-time $500 home office stipend, requested through the HR portal within the first 60 days of start date or of switching to a remote role
- A recurring $50 per month internet stipend, added automatically to payroll, no separate request needed

The home office stipend covers desks, chairs, monitors, and similar equipment. It does not cover the laptop itself, which comes from IT under the laptop policy, and is not intended for consumables like printer paper.

## Requesting an exception

If your role is technically hybrid but your work is genuinely location-independent (for example, a specialist role with no local team), talk to your manager about a remote exception. Exceptions need sign-off from your VP and are noted in Workday so attendance tracking reflects the agreed arrangement.

## Dress code

Business casual applies on office days. There is no dress code requirement for remote work days, video calls included, beyond basic professionalism (no exceptions for client-facing calls, where business casual still applies).

## Attendance expectations

Hybrid employees who are consistently absent on required in-office days without an agreed exception should expect a conversation with their manager. This is handled locally between manager and employee in the first instance rather than through a formal process, unless the pattern continues after that conversation.

## Travel between offices

Employees traveling between Austin, Toronto, and Lisbon for work purposes (team offsites, cross-office collaboration) follow the standard travel policy for booking and expenses. This is separate from the hybrid schedule and does not count against your regular in-office days at your home location.

## International remote work

Working temporarily from a country other than your registered work location (for example, a few weeks with family abroad) needs advance approval from Ruth Adeyemi's team for tax and legal reasons, requested at least 2 weeks ahead through the HR portal. Unapproved extended work from another country can create tax withholding and legal problems for both the employee and the company, so this rule is enforced consistently regardless of role or tenure.

## FAQ

**Can I permanently relocate to a different city?** Yes, but it needs to go through Workday as a formal move, since it can change your office assignment, tax setup, and in some cases compensation band. Talk to Aisha Karim's team before finalizing any lease or relocation.

**Does the internet stipend apply to Lisbon staff even though they aren't required in an office?** Yes, the stipend is based on remote or hybrid status, not on how many days you happen to be in office.

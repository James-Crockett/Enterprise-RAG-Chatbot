# Learning and development budget

Last reviewed: 2026-01-25 by Aisha Karim, Head of People

## Budget amount

Every employee gets an annual learning budget of $1,500, refreshed each calendar year on January 1. Unused budget does not roll over into the next year, and there is no partial-year proration for employees who start mid-year; a new hire starting any time in 2026 gets the full $1,500 for the remainder of that calendar year.

## What it covers

- Courses and certifications relevant to your role (technical certifications, project management, design tools, and similar)
- Conference tickets and associated travel, subject to the standard travel policy for booking
- Books, either physical or digital, relevant to your role
- Professional membership fees (for example, an engineering association or a professional certification body)

## What it does not cover

- General interest courses unrelated to your role
- Equipment or software (request those through the IT portal instead, under the software request process)
- Coaching or therapy (this is covered separately under the wellness benefit, not the learning budget)

## How to use it

1. Submit the request in the HR portal under "Learning and development," describing the course or conference and its cost.
2. Your manager approves within 3 business days. No further approval is needed if the total cost is under $1,500 and your remaining budget covers it.
3. Pay for the item using Expensify as you would any other expense, tagging it "learning budget" so finance can track it against your allocation.
4. Submit the Expensify receipt within 30 days of purchase, same as the general expense policy.

## Exceeding the budget

If a course or conference costs more than your remaining budget for the year, you can request an exception through your manager and Aisha Karim's team. Exceptions are considered case by case, weighing the relevance to your role and team budget, and are not guaranteed.

## Team and department training

Some training is organized at the team level rather than through individual budgets, for example a department-wide security certification for engineering or a sales methodology course for the whole sales team under Jordan Pike. This comes out of the department's own training budget, not individual $1,500 allocations, and is arranged directly by the manager.

## Internal learning resources

Kestrel also maintains an internal learning library in Confluence with recorded internal talks, onboarding materials, and role-specific playbooks. These are free to everyone and don't draw against the personal learning budget.

## Manager development

Managers get an additional $500 per year specifically for management training (coaching certifications, leadership programs), separate from and in addition to their standard $1,500 individual learning budget.

## FAQ

**Can I use the budget on something for a friend or family member, like a course we take together?** No, the budget covers only your own professional development.

**What happens to my budget if I go on parental leave partway through the year?** It's unaffected. The full annual amount stays available regardless of leave taken during the year.

**Does the budget cover a college degree program?** Generally no, tuition assistance for degree programs is a separate benefit; talk to Aisha Karim's team about what's available for that.

# Code of conduct

Last reviewed: 2026-02-15 by Ruth Adeyemi, General Counsel

## Purpose

This code sets expectations for behavior at Kestrel Systems, in the office, remote, and at company events. It applies to every employee, contractor, and intern, regardless of location or work model.

## Core expectations

1. Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Disagreement on ideas is normal and encouraged; personal attacks are not.
2. Do the work you're paid for honestly. Do not misrepresent hours worked, expenses, or the state of a project.
3. Protect confidential information, including customer data, unreleased product plans, and internal financial figures. Confidentiality obligations continue after you leave Kestrel.
4. Follow the law and company policy in your work, including data privacy rules relevant to customer fleets and drivers whose information passes through Kestrel Route.
5. Disclose conflicts of interest, such as a financial stake in a vendor or competitor, or a close personal relationship with someone you manage or who manages you.

## Harassment and discrimination

Kestrel does not tolerate harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, or any other protected characteristic. This includes:

- Unwelcome comments or jokes targeting a protected characteristic
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith
- Sexual harassment, including unwanted advances or comments, whether in person, on Slack, or at a company event

## Reporting a concern

Report a concern through one of these channels:

1. Your manager, if you're comfortable raising it directly
2. Aisha Karim's team, for anything related to workplace conduct
3. Ruth Adeyemi's team, for anything with legal implications or if you're not comfortable going through HR
4. The anonymous ethics line, linked from the HR portal, for anonymous reports

Reports are investigated promptly and kept confidential to the extent possible given the need to investigate. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith, even if the investigation doesn't substantiate the concern, is itself a violation of this code.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any of the following to your manager and Ruth Adeyemi's team:

- Outside employment or consulting, especially for a competitor or customer
- Financial interest in a vendor, customer, or competitor beyond a small holding in a public company
- Hiring or managing a close family member or romantic partner

Disclosure does not automatically block the arrangement, but it needs to be reviewed and, where needed, managed (for example, removing yourself from a hiring decision involving a family member).

## Use of company systems

Company systems (email, Slack, GitHub, laptops) are for business use, with reasonable personal use allowed. Do not use company systems to harass others, to conduct outside business, or to access or share data outside your role's normal scope.

## Gifts and entertainment

Modest gifts and meals from vendors or customers, in the range normal for the business relationship, are fine. Anything beyond that, or any cash or cash-equivalent gift, needs disclosure to Ruth Adeyemi's team before accepting.

## Consequences

Violations of this code range from a documented conversation to termination, depending on severity and history. Serious violations, including harassment, discrimination, fraud, and confidentiality breaches, can result in immediate termination regardless of tenure or role.

## FAQ

**Does this apply to interactions at an off-site company event?** Yes, the code applies at any company-sponsored event, not just in the office.

**What if my manager is the one I want to report?** Go to Aisha Karim's team or Ruth Adeyemi's team directly, or use the anonymous ethics line.

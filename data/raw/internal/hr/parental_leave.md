# Parental leave policy

Last reviewed: 2026-01-10 by Aisha Karim, Head of People

## Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for parental leave from their first day of employment. There is no minimum tenure requirement, unlike some other benefits that phase in over time.

## Leave duration

| Role in the birth or adoption | Paid leave |
| --- | --- |
| Birthing parent | 16 weeks |
| Non-birthing parent or adoptive parent | 10 weeks |

Leave is paid at 100 percent of base salary for the full duration listed above. It does not count against regular PTO, which continues to accrue separately during the leave.

## Requesting leave

1. Notify your manager and Aisha Karim's team as soon as you have a target date, ideally at least 8 weeks ahead for a planned leave.
2. Submit the formal leave request in the HR portal, which routes to HR for processing rather than manager approval, given the fixed entitlement.
3. HR confirms the leave dates and pay setup in writing within 5 business days of the request.
4. For unplanned situations (early birth, sudden adoption placement), notify your manager and HR as soon as reasonably possible, and the same process applies retroactively.

## Timing and flexibility

Leave can start up to 2 weeks before an expected birth date and must be used within 12 months of the birth or placement. It can be taken continuously or split into two blocks (for example, an initial block after birth and a second block later in the first year), agreed in advance with HR.

## Health benefits during leave

Health, dental, and vision coverage continue unchanged during parental leave, with Kestrel covering its normal share of premiums. There is no gap or requirement to pay premiums differently than during regular employment.

## 401(k) and other benefits

401(k) contributions and the company match up to 4% continue as normal if the employee keeps contributing from their paid leave salary. PTO continues to accrue each pay period during leave, same as regular employment.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave return to their same role, or an equivalent role at the same level and compensation if the original position was restructured while they were out. Managers should coordinate with Aisha Karim's team at least 2 weeks before the planned return date to plan onboarding back into current projects.

## Phased return

Employees can request a phased return (reduced hours for up to 4 weeks) through HR. This is agreed case by case with the manager and does not change the total paid leave entitlement, since it starts after the standard leave period ends.

## Leave for pregnancy loss or complications

Employees experiencing pregnancy loss, or medical complications related to pregnancy or birth, are eligible for additional paid medical leave beyond the standard entitlement, coordinated through HR and, where relevant, short-term disability. Talk to Aisha Karim's team directly; this is handled with discretion and does not require details beyond what the employee is comfortable sharing.

## FAQ

**Does this apply to same-sex couples and adoptive parents equally?** Yes, the 10-week non-birthing parent entitlement applies regardless of family structure or how the child joined the family.

**Can leave be taken part-time throughout instead of as a block?** Not under the standard policy. Talk to HR about a phased return instead, which covers the transition period after the leave block.

# Travel policy

Last reviewed: 2026-02-05 by Elena Vasquez, CFO

## Booking travel

Book flights and hotels as far in advance as practical, ideally at least 14 days ahead for domestic trips and 21 days for international trips, to keep costs reasonable. Book directly and submit the receipt through Expensify rather than through a separate travel booking tool, since Kestrel does not currently mandate a single booking platform.

## Flights

| Flight duration | Class |
| --- | --- |
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| Over 6 hours | Premium economy allowed |

Business class is not covered under standard policy. An exception for a specific health need or an unusually long itinerary (for example, more than two connecting long-haul segments) can be requested from your VP and finance in advance, not after booking.

## Hotels

Standard hotel limit is $250 per night. In New York, San Francisco, and London, where hotel costs run higher, the limit is $325 per night. If a conference hotel is the only reasonable option and exceeds the limit, note the reason in Expensify when submitting.

## Meals while traveling

The $75 per day meal limit from the expense policy applies while traveling, same as any other business day. This covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined, plus reasonable incidental food and drink.

## Ground transportation

Airport transfers, rideshares, and local transit for business purposes are reimbursable. Renting a car is reimbursable when it's clearly cheaper or more practical than rideshare for the trip (for example, visiting multiple customer sites in one day in a city without good rideshare coverage).

## Cross-office travel

Travel between the Austin, Toronto, and Lisbon offices for team collaboration or offsites follows this same policy for booking and limits. It's arranged with your manager and, for anything involving more than a few employees traveling together, coordinated with Aisha Karim's team for scheduling.

## Trips over $5,000

A trip or connected set of bookings (flights, hotel, conference registration) that together exceed $5,000 needs procurement review through Coupa before booking, same as any other large purchase under the expense policy. This most commonly comes up for larger conferences or a group of employees attending the same event.

## International travel

Check with Ruth Adeyemi's team before international travel if the trip involves any work on customer premises, since some countries have specific requirements for business visas versus tourist entry. This is separate from and in addition to standard trip approval from your manager.

## Travel insurance and safety

Kestrel provides travel insurance automatically for any trip booked and expensed as company travel. If you run into a safety issue while traveling (illness, a canceled flight stranding you, a natural event affecting your location), contact your manager and, outside office hours, the emergency line listed in the HR portal.

## Combining business and personal travel

Extending a business trip for personal time is fine as long as any extra flight cost from the personal dates is not billed to the company. Compare the fare for the business-only dates against the actual booked fare and expense only the lower of the two.

## FAQ

**Do I need pre-approval for every business trip?** Yes, a manager needs to approve the trip before booking, even if the total cost is well under any policy limit.

**Can I book a nicer hotel and pay the difference myself?** Yes, book what you want and expense only up to the applicable nightly limit, covering the rest personally.

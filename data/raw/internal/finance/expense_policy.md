# Expense policy

Last reviewed: 2026-02-05 by Elena Vasquez, CFO

## Submitting expenses

All business expenses are submitted through Expensify. Use the Expensify card issued to you for business purchases where possible, and submit the receipt and category in the app within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses submitted after 30 days need manager sign-off explaining the delay and may still be declined at finance's discretion.

## Standard limits

| Category | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Meals | $75 per day |
| Hotels (standard cities) | $250 per night |
| Hotels (New York, San Francisco, London) | $325 per night |
| Flights under 6 hours | Economy |
| Flights over 6 hours | Premium economy allowed |

Meal limits apply per day, not per meal, so a lighter breakfast can offset a business dinner within the same $75 cap. Alcohol at a business meal is reimbursable in moderation but is not reimbursable when drinking alone.

## Approval workflow

1. Submit the expense in Expensify with a receipt attached and the correct category selected.
2. Your manager approves within 5 business days for standard expenses.
3. Anything unclear or over policy limits is flagged automatically to finance for a manual review before reimbursement.

## What's reimbursable

- Client meals and reasonable team meals while traveling
- Hotel and flight costs booked for business travel
- Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, public transit) to and from business activities
- Reasonable Wi-Fi charges while traveling for work

## What's not reimbursable

- Personal entertainment unrelated to business purpose
- Airline seat upgrades beyond premium economy, or premium economy on flights under 6 hours
- Traffic tickets, parking fines, or other penalties incurred while traveling
- Alcohol purchased for personal consumption outside a business meal context

## Purchases over $5,000

Any purchase over $5,000, whether a single item or a bundled order, does not go through Expensify. It goes through Coupa and requires procurement review before the purchase is made, not after. Submitting a large purchase through Expensify after the fact, without prior procurement approval, is treated as a policy violation regardless of whether the purchase itself was reasonable.

## Expensify cards

Everyone gets an Expensify card for business spending. Personal purchases on the Expensify card are not permitted; if one happens by mistake, flag it in the app immediately and repay it through the reconciliation process rather than letting it sit unresolved.

## International expenses

Expenses in a foreign currency are converted automatically by Expensify at the rate on the transaction date. There's no need to manually convert or explain exchange rate differences in the submission.

## Non-compliant expenses

An expense over the standard limit isn't automatically rejected, but it needs a written explanation in the Expensify note field (for example, "no hotel available under $325 during a major conference in San Francisco"). Repeated over-limit submissions without justification are reviewed by finance with the employee's manager.

## FAQ

**Can I get reimbursed for a home internet upgrade?** No, ongoing home office costs are covered under the $50/month internet stipend in the hybrid and remote work policy, not through expense submissions.

**Who do I ask if Expensify rejects something I think should be covered?** Reply on the same submission with your reasoning. Persistent disagreements go to your manager and finance together.

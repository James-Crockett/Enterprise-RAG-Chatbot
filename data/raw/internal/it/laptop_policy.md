# Laptop policy

Last reviewed: 2026-01-15 by Sam Ortiz, IT lead

## Standard hardware

Kestrel issues a laptop to every employee based on role.

| Role | Standard laptop |
| --- | --- |
| Engineering | MacBook Pro 14 inch |
| All other roles | MacBook Air |

Requests for a different configuration (larger screen, more RAM, a second machine) go through the IT portal and need Marcus Lee's sign-off for engineering roles, or your VP's sign-off for other departments.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops refresh every 3 years from the original issue date, tracked in Kandji. IT emails you 60 days before your refresh date to schedule a swap. If your laptop is having performance problems well before the 3-year mark, file a ticket rather than waiting, since IT can approve an early refresh case by case.

## Enrollment and management

Every company laptop enrolls in Kandji on first boot. Kandji pushes:

1. Disk encryption (FileVault), enabled automatically and required before the device can access internal systems.
2. Standard security baseline, including screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
3. Approved application catalog, so employees can self-install common tools without a ticket.
4. OS updates, applied automatically within 14 days of release unless IT delays a specific update for compatibility reasons.

Do not remove a laptop from Kandji management. A device that drops out of Kandji loses access to Okta-protected apps until it is re-enrolled.

## Loss or damage

Report a lost or stolen laptop to the IT portal immediately, marked urgent, so IT can remote-wipe it through Kandji. This applies at any hour, not just business hours, given the wipe needs to happen fast.

Accidental damage (spills, drops, cracked screens) is covered under AppleCare on all company devices. Bring the laptop to the nearest office IT desk in Austin, Toronto, or Lisbon, or ship it to Austin if you work remotely, and IT arranges the repair.

## Personal use and departure

Company laptops are for work use. Reasonable personal use (checking personal email, browsing) is fine, but do not install personal software that requires admin rights without going through the IT portal first.

When an employee leaves, IT collects the laptop on the last day, or issues a prepaid shipping label for remote employees to return it within 5 business days. The laptop wipes and re-images before the next assignment.

## Peripherals

New hires can request a monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset through the IT portal at no additional approval. Requests for standing desks or ergonomic chairs go through Aisha Karim's team as part of the home office stipend process, not the IT portal.

## FAQ

**Can I use my own laptop instead of a company one?** No. All internal systems require an enrolled, encrypted company device.

**Who do I contact if Kandji blocks an app I need for work?** File a ticket in the IT portal describing the business need. IT reviews within 24 hours on business days.

**Does the refresh cycle reset if I get a replacement after damage?** No, the replacement keeps the original issue date unless the device is more than 2 years old at the time of replacement.

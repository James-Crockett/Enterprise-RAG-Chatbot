# Roadmap process

Last reviewed: 2026-01-15

Owner: Nina Castellanos, Head of Product.

## Cadence

Roadmap planning runs on a quarterly cycle. Customer facing release notes go out monthly, but the roadmap itself, meaning what gets prioritized and staffed, is set once per quarter and adjusted only for urgent security or reliability issues in between.

The planning cycle for a given quarter starts about 3 weeks before the quarter begins, so Q3 planning (July through September) starts in early June.

## Inputs

1. Productboard, where feature requests from Customer Success and Sales are logged and voted on by account value.
2. Gong tagged competitive mentions, reviewed with Jordan Pike's team (see Competitive Notes).
3. Support escalation trends from the past quarter, pulled by Tom Brennan's and the support team's Jira data.
4. Engineering capacity, confirmed by Marcus Lee based on current headcount and any planned time off for the team leads.
5. Direct input from Dana Okafor and Priya Raman on strategic bets, usually 1 to 2 items per quarter that do not need to come through Productboard voting.

## Process

Nina Castellanos's team compiles a ranked list from Productboard plus the other inputs above, weighted roughly 40 percent customer request volume and revenue impact, 30 percent support and reliability signals, 20 percent competitive gaps, 10 percent strategic bets from leadership.

A draft roadmap is reviewed in a 90 minute meeting with Nina Castellanos, Marcus Lee, Priya Raman, and Jordan Pike. Elena Vasquez joins if any item has a material cost implication (new AWS region, new third party integration with license fees). Dana Okafor reviews and signs off on the final quarterly roadmap before it is shared company wide in the all hands.

## What goes on the roadmap vs. the backlog

An item goes on the quarterly roadmap if it is expected to ship, or make meaningful progress, within that quarter and has an assigned engineering lead. Items that are still being scoped, or that need more customer validation, stay in the Productboard backlog and get revisited the following quarter.

## Module ownership

Each product module has a named product owner responsible for that module's roadmap items:

| Module | Product owner |
|---|---|
| Route Planner | Nina Castellanos |
| Live Dispatch | Product manager reporting to Nina Castellanos, module lead rotates by project |
| Driver App | Product manager reporting to Nina Castellanos |
| Proof of Delivery | Product manager reporting to Nina Castellanos |
| Analytics | Product manager reporting to Nina Castellanos, works closely with Marcus Lee's data team |

## Communicating changes

Roadmap items that slip or get cut mid quarter are communicated by Nina Castellanos in the #product-updates Slack channel within 2 business days of the decision, with a short reason. Sales and Customer Success are notified separately if the change affects a commitment already made to a specific customer, since those commitments need a direct conversation, not just a Slack post.

## Customer commitments

Sales reps should not promise a specific ship date for an unreleased feature without checking with Nina Castellanos's team first. If a prospect's deal depends on a roadmap item, log it in Productboard with the opportunity linked, so it is visible during quarterly planning.
